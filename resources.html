Open-source packages

  • gold-melt-value (Go package)

    Pure, dependency-free Go math that computes precious-metal melt value from karat, weight, and a live spot price. The engine behind GoldGramPrice.

    Also indexed on docs.rs · lib.rs · source
  • dscr-ratio (Go package)

    Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) for rental-property loans: NOI ÷ annual debt service. The engine behind DSCRRadar.

    Also indexed on docs.rs · lib.rs · source
  • us-paycheck-tax (Rust crate)

    2025 US federal income tax (progressive brackets plus standard deduction) and FICA take-home math. The engine behind StateWage.

    Also on crates.io · source
  • lake-elevation (Rust crate)

    Reservoir and lake elevation math: percent-of-pool, storage conversions, and datum conversions. The engine behind LakeLevelNow.

    Also on crates.io · source
  • uk-paye (Rust crate)

    2025/26 UK income tax bands for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, the personal-allowance taper, and National Insurance. The engine behind UKMoneyCalc.

    Also on crates.io · source
  • amortization-schedule (Rust crate)

    Loan amortization: monthly payment, per-period breakdown, total interest, and remaining balance. The engine behind AssetLoanCalculator.

    Also on crates.io · source
  • recipe-ratio (Rust crate)

    Baker's percentages, dough hydration, and recipe scaling by flour weight. The engine behind IngredientCalculator.

    Also on crates.io · source

Datasets

  • Dataset & reference-data repositories

    Open repositories of the reference data behind these calculators (tax brackets, lake-level feeds, spot-price series, HS tariff tables). Zenodo DOI deposits for the long-running series are published from these repos as they stabilize.

    Canonical datasets are versioned in their source repos above; this listing will link individual Zenodo DOIs as each is minted.

Notes and explainers

  • Working notes blog

    Longer write-ups behind the calculators: how full-pool elevation differs from depth, why the personal-allowance taper creates a 60 percent marginal band, why DSCR moves faster on loan term than on rent, and which OBBBA provisions expire. Each post works from the published reference data rather than summarising it.

    Hosted on mataroa.blog. Posts are written by hand; figures are recomputed from the source datasets at publication.
